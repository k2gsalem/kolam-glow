Kolam Glow — Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026 (version 0.2 and later)

Kolam Glow has no account and no sign-in, and keeps your game on your phone.

  • Your levels, stars, streak, hints, settings and finished kolams are saved only on your phone. If you have turned on Android backup, Android may include them in your backup, under Google's privacy policy.
  • When you tap Share, the picture of your kolam goes only to the app you choose.

Premium

If you buy Premium, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. Google tells the app only that Premium is owned.

Reward videos

Videos play only when you choose to watch one for a hint. They come from Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, your approximate location (from your IP address) and how ads perform, as described in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where the law asks for it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.

Children

Before the first video or purchase, the app asks for your birth year. The year stays on your phone. If you are under 13, videos are child-directed: no personalised ads, no ad tracking, family-friendly (G-rated) ads only, and a grown-up must answer a question before any purchase. Everyone else also sees only G-rated ads.

We do not run our own servers or analytics, and we never sell your information.
