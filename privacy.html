Kolam Glow — Privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026

Kolam Glow does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • The app does not use the internet. It has no internet permission, no ads and no analytics.
  • Your levels, stars, streak and finished kolams are saved only on your phone. If you have turned on Android backup, Android may include them in your backup, under Google's privacy policy.
  • When you tap Share, the picture of your kolam goes only to the app you choose.
  • Kolam Glow is for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

If a future version adds ads or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
